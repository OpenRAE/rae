# ASR-538 — Modular Participant-Control Realization Conformance

## Statement

RAES shall publish independently runnable bounded conformance for exact
participant-control profiles, mechanism compositions, provider protocols and
governed effects through real final participant/world crossings. Evidence shall
pin backend, apparatus, mechanism, profile, implementation, configuration,
authority, policy, memory, cut, effect and limitation identities, and distinguish
semantic publication, contract validity, runtime orchestration, executable
provider, declaration, realization, conformance and experimental claims.
Positive and negative probes shall cover permissive influence observation,
strict denial with zero prohibited effect, triggered injects and other supported
effects, non-IFC composition, absence, unsupported and weakened support,
conflicts, stale cuts, failure, bounded triggers and supported recovery.
Independent backends may conform to different declared subsets without implied
mechanism parity, behavioral equivalence or universal assurance. Out-of-world
integrity failures shall invalidate realization rather than become in-world
attack occurrences.

## Rationale

ASR-535 owns bounded flow assurance and ASR-536 owns intentional-subversion
evaluation. Neither is a substitute for independent modular provider/effect
conformance. ASR-537 continues to own mixed-backend realization/transfer claims.

## Fulfillment boundary

DRAFT through #1068. #1071 publishes the reusable corpus and runner after
#1070, #1072 and #1069. LilRAE #22 and Shifter #1969 separately establish real
installed backend evidence; no fixture provider establishes that evidence.
